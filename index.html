The problem

Every new face means starting over

Support workers change often, sometimes every week. A minimally verbal child can't tell a new person that a certain sound means they need the toilet, or that a busy room has become too much.

So families explain it all again, and much of what they know gets lost between shifts.

How it works

Everything a new worker needs, in the family's own words

  • Before the shift

    A first-shift card

    The child's cues for toileting, food and overstimulation, and what calms them. Clear enough to read in two minutes.

  • Who can see it

    Family-controlled access

    Families choose who can see the profile. Access is time-limited and expires when the shift ends.

  • After every shift

    Gets smarter every shift

    Workers log what worked. Families review it and approve what goes into the profile, so it stays up to date.

Who it's for
